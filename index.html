01 — SAGI Finance Product / Android / Web

A finance product built from the ground up.

SAGI Finance is a personal finance application built to make everyday financial tracking simple and practical. It brings income tracking, expense tracking, budgets and a clear view of financial activity into a single web application, extended to Android through a Trusted Web Activity and backed by Firebase, with its own subscription infrastructure.
